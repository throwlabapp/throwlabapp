Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

ThrowLab is a disc golf analysis app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app accesses, why it needs that access, and where your information goes. The short version: throw analysis runs on your iPhone. Your videos are not uploaded for analysis, and no ThrowLab account is required.

Information we collect

ThrowLab has no user accounts or advertising SDKs. ThrowLab does not collect or store your name, email address, throw videos, tracked frames, measurements, or analysis results on ThrowLab servers. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect limited app-usage information as described below.

Camera and video

ThrowLab may request camera access so you can record a throw. Recorded video is stored locally and analyzed on the device with Apple’s Vision framework. ThrowLab does not upload or transmit your video.

Photo library

ThrowLab requests photo-library access so you can select an existing slow-motion throw video. Only the video you choose is read. The app does not scan or upload your photo library.

Motion data

The app may read device-motion data during capture to support analysis. This information is used locally and is not transmitted to ThrowLab.

Data stored on your device

  • Throw recordings and analysis results
  • Discs saved in your bag and their ratings
  • Local diagnostic information associated with your analyses
  • App settings and preferences

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. ThrowLab cannot recover information that was never sent to a server.

Disc catalog lookup

When you search for a disc to add to your bag, ThrowLab may request matching public disc information from a catalog service. This lookup uses the disc name you type. Your throw videos and analysis results are not included.

Subscriptions and payments

Apple processes ThrowLab Pro subscriptions through the App Store. ThrowLab does not receive your card number or billing address. Apple’s handling of purchases is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

App analytics

ThrowLab uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve its features, reliability, and subscription experience. Firebase may collect an anonymous app-instance identifier, app version, device and operating-system information, approximate geographic information derived from an IP address, app lifecycle information, subscription or purchase-event information, and interactions with app features.

ThrowLab may record events such as opening a feature, beginning or completing an analysis, adding a disc, using Compare, viewing Sessions or Milestones, opening the subscription screen, and completing a purchase. ThrowLab does not send Firebase your videos, video frames, tracked coordinates, disc names, search terms, throw selections, measurements, or analysis results. Those remain stored and processed on your device.

App analytics is used to understand feature adoption, identify where users leave the analysis process, improve the app, and evaluate subscription conversion. ThrowLab uses the Firebase Analytics configuration without advertising-identifier capability. Analytics information is not sold or used by ThrowLab for cross-app tracking or personalized advertising. Google processes this information according to its privacy and security terms.

Website analytics and cookies

The ThrowLab website uses Google Tag Manager and, with your permission, Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, navigation, and interactions such as App Store link clicks. Analytics storage is denied until you select Accept analytics in the consent banner. If you decline, analytics storage remains disabled. Your choice is saved in your browser so the website can remember it. You can reopen Cookie settings from the website footer at any time.

Google may process analytics information according to Google’s Privacy Policy. ThrowLab does not use website analytics to access your throw videos or in-app analysis results.

Tracking

The ThrowLab iPhone app does not track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or share information with data brokers or advertisers. Firebase associates app analytics with an anonymous app-instance identifier so activity from one installation can be understood across sessions. This identifier is not a ThrowLab account and is not connected by ThrowLab to your name or email address. Website analytics is optional and controlled by the consent choice described above.

Children

ThrowLab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no account system, children should use it only with appropriate supervision during physical activity.

Your choices

You control the videos you select and the information stored locally in the app. You can delete saved items in ThrowLab or delete the app to remove its local data. There is no ThrowLab account or named server-side profile. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new revision date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@throwlabapp.com.
