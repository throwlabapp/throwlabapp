How to measure disc golf throw distance without GPS drift

Walk from where you threw to where your disc landed, and ThrowLab gives you the distance. It doesn’t rely on a GPS dot, so a throw in the woods reads as steady as one in an open field.

Try measuring a drive on a wooded hole with a GPS app and you’ll watch the blue dot wander. Tree cover can throw a phone’s position off by many feet, and both the start and the end of your throw are affected. That’s good enough to find the basket and not steady enough to compare one throw with another.

The other ways to measure

Pacing it off is quick and works on a flat field with a steady stride. Over rough ground and slopes, your stride changes and the number goes with it. A rangefinder is accurate when you can get a clean shot at the disc, which is hard when it’s in the rough, behind a tree, or over a rise. A measuring wheel is accurate and a nuisance to carry and walk every time.

Each of these works some of the time. The best distance method is the one you’ll still be using after the third round, and that’s why most people end up guessing.

Measuring a throw in ThrowLab

  1. Choose the disc in ThrowLab and tap Measure Distance.
  2. Stand where you threw and tap to start.
  3. Hold your phone in front of you, with the back camera uncovered, and walk to your disc.
  4. Tap to finish when you’re beside it.

You don’t record or upload a video for this. The distance saves with the disc, so your farthest throw for each disc shows up in Milestones, and distance trends show up under Trends.

Wind and elevation

A throw into a headwind and a throw downhill aren’t the same throw, even at the same distance. Each distance comes with an estimated elevation change and wind, so you can tell a strong throw in bad conditions from an average one in good conditions. They’re estimates, and they’re meant to give context, not to correct the number.

Getting numbers you can compare

Measure the same disc on similar ground. Throw several, because a single throw tells you little. If your best throws always come downwind, take that into account when you compare.

Distance tells you how far a throw went and nothing about how it got there. When the distance surprises you, run the flight analysis on the next throw and check speed, disc tilt, and launch angle. Here’s how to read them.

Questions people ask

Can I measure distance in a net?

No. A net stops every throw short. Use the net to work on flight and take distance outside.

Does distance work under trees?

Yes, and that’s the main reason to use it. It doesn’t depend on a GPS dot, so tree cover doesn’t distort it.

Do I need to record video for distance?

No. Video is only for flight analysis.
