Flight analysis

Speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from one slow-motion throw.

Film a throw in slow motion and ThrowLab gives you speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from your release and early flight. Each reading sits next to your earlier throws with that disc and throwing style, so you know whether it was your usual or something new. Record right in the app or upload a clip you already have.

SpeedHow fast the disc is moving, next to your usual for that disc and style.
Disc tiltWhether the rim is tilted down, flat, or up as the disc leaves your hand and starts flying.
Launch angleWhether the disc is heading up, level, or down early in its flight.

Recording setup

Give the camera a clear view.

Film from the side in good light, in 240 fps slow motion. ThrowLab reads your release and early flight, so keep the disc in the shot after it leaves your hand.

240 fpsSlow-motion video
LandscapeRoom for the throw and early flight
Side viewHand and disc edge in view
Early flightKeep the disc visible after release

The longer the disc stays in clear view, the better your reading. Set the camera up the same way each time so your throws compare fairly.

Frequently asked questions

Why is ThrowLab’s distance more accurate than a GPS app?

ThrowLab’s distance doesn’t drift the way a GPS reading does. Phone GPS can wander by many feet, especially under trees, while ThrowLab gives the same steady reading in the woods as in an open field.

How accurate is the distance tracker?

ThrowLab has been field tested to within one foot of the reference distance, including on wooded courses.

How is ThrowLab different from a sensor disc or launch monitor?

ThrowLab is an app, not hardware. Nothing gets attached to your discs, so you can measure and compare your whole bag while every disc stays stock. It also measures distance and keeps a history for each disc.

Is there a disc golf launch monitor app for iPhone?

Yes. ThrowLab gives you speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from a slow-motion video of your throw, with no hardware.

Can I measure my throw speed without a radar or sensor disc?

Yes. ThrowLab estimates speed from slow-motion video of your release and early flight. Its speed readings have been tested against multiple radar devices, with results within a few mph.

Do I need a special disc or extra hardware?

No. ThrowLab works with every disc in your bag. There’s no sensor to buy or attach, and nothing to modify.

What iPhone do I need?

iOS 17 or later, and an iPhone that records 240 fps slow motion. You can record in ThrowLab or upload a slow-motion clip from Photos. Distance measurement works on any supported iPhone.

Can I record directly in ThrowLab?

Yes. Film the throw in the app, or upload a slow-motion clip from Photos.

What do disc tilt and launch angle mean?

Disc tilt is how far the disc’s rim is tilted up or down as it leaves your hand and starts its flight. Launch angle is whether the disc is heading up, level, or down early in its flight. A tilted disc can still launch level.

What part of the throw does ThrowLab measure?

Your release and early flight. Because ThrowLab keeps reading the disc after it leaves your hand, its numbers can differ slightly from devices that only measure at release.

Can I practice into a net?

Yes. Leave some room in front of the net so the disc stays in view for a moment after release. Backyard, field, and course throws all work too.

Does it work for forehand and left-handed throws?

Yes. Choose forehand or backhand, right- or left-handed, before you analyze.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Throw videos are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded for analysis. No account is required.

How much does ThrowLab cost?

Your first 5 analyses are free, flight or distance, with no account. After that, ThrowLab Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year for unlimited analyses.
