Quick checks

Before running the analysis again

Use Slo-mo mode

Record at 240 fps in your iPhone’s Slo-mo camera mode, then import the clip into ThrowLab.

Film from the side

Keep the phone roughly perpendicular to the intended throwing line and use landscape orientation.

Trim to the throw

Begin just before the disc leaves your hand and end shortly after the disc exits the frame. Long wind-ups or follow-throughs can make the starting frame less useful.

Choose mid-flight

On the Find the disc screen, scrub until the disc is out of the hand, visible, and roughly halfway across its flight through the frame.

Distance tracking

Help with measuring a throw

How do I track distance?

Select your disc, throwing style, and intended line, then tap Track distance. Allow Camera access and tap Start where you throw. Keep the app open and hold the phone in front of you with the back camera uncovered as you walk to your disc. Tap Finish when you reach it.

Why is tracking not ready?

Check Camera access in iPhone Settings. Keep the back camera uncovered and follow the on-screen instructions. Allow Location access for local wind information.

Can I switch apps or lock my phone?

Keep ThrowLab open throughout the measurement. If tracking is interrupted, follow the on-screen instructions; you may need to restart at the throwing position.

What does the distance include?

The distance runs straight from where you threw to where the disc stopped, including skips and rolls. Available elevation and wind readings appear with the result.

Why did a measurement not count?

The app flags measurements that fall outside its tracking or distance limits. These stay in Sessions but do not count toward records, averages, or Trends.

How do I remove an incorrect measurement?

Use Delete throw on the distance result, or swipe the throw in Sessions and confirm Delete. Sessions and distance records update when a measurement is removed.

Analysis and billing

More help with ThrowLab

Why is a reading missing?

Choose a frame where the disc is out of your hand and its edge is clearly visible. If a reading is still missing, try another frame or record in better light. Missing readings appear as a dash and are left out of Trends.

What do the disc-angle labels mean?

Readings from −8° through +8° are labeled Flat. In Trends, angles below −8° indicate anhyzer and angles above +8° indicate hyzer. Result cards show the angle with a Flat, Hyzer, or Anhyzer label.

Why can’t I find a disc?

Try the disc or manufacturer name. ThrowLab has an on-device catalog and can refresh search results online. If a disc is unavailable, enter its details and flight numbers manually. Discs already saved in your bag remain available.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

To cancel, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore an active subscription after reinstalling, use Restore Purchases in ThrowLab while signed into the same Apple Account.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes all purchases and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

How many free uses do I get?

New users get 5 completed video analyses and 5 saved distance measurements, counted separately. Deleting a throw does not restore a free use. Pro unlocks unlimited access to both. You can still view results you already saved.
