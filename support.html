Common questions

ThrowLab couldn’t find my disc in the video. What went wrong?

Almost always a capture issue. Shoot landscape from the side, keep the whole throwing motion in frame, and trim the clip tightly around the throw. Slow-motion (120fps or 240fps) gives the tracker far more frames to lock onto, and good even lighting matters more than resolution.

How should I film a throw?

Stand roughly perpendicular to your intended line, about 15–25 feet away, phone in landscape at chest height. Record in slo-mo, then trim to just before the reach-back through a beat after release.

Why do two throws with different discs get different feedback?

That’s intentional. Analysis is matched to the speed, glide, turn, and fade of the disc you selected from your bag. The same release that’s perfect for a mid-range is under-powered for a high-speed driver.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis runs on your iPhone using Apple’s Vision framework. There are no accounts and no ThrowLab servers.

How do I cancel or restore a ThrowLab Pro subscription?

Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore on a new or reinstalled device, tap “Restore Purchases” in the app while signed into the same Apple Account.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not ThrowLab. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
