Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

ThrowLab (“the app”) is a disc golf throw analysis app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. The short version: everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. ThrowLab has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. There is no ThrowLab server that your data is sent to.

Camera and video

ThrowLab requests camera access so you can record a throw directly in the app. Recorded video is written to your device’s local app storage and analyzed on device using Apple’s Vision framework. Video is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted off your iPhone by ThrowLab.

Photo library

ThrowLab requests photo library access so you can import an existing slow-motion throw video for analysis. Only the video you explicitly select is read. The app does not scan, index, or upload your library.

Motion data

The app may read device motion sensor data during capture to help stabilize and calibrate the analysis. This data is used only in the moment, stored locally alongside the recording if needed for replay, and never transmitted.

Data stored on your device

  • Throw recordings and their analysis results
  • Your bag — the discs you have added and their ratings
  • Calibration and diagnostic data used to improve accuracy of your own analyses
  • App settings and preferences

All of this lives in ThrowLab’s local storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

Subscriptions and payments

ThrowLab Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. ThrowLab never sees or stores your payment details, card number, or billing address. Purchase and entitlement status is validated through Apple’s StoreKit on your device. Apple’s handling of that transaction is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Tracking

ThrowLab does not track you. We do not collect data linked to your identity, we do not share data with data brokers or advertisers, and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency permission because there is nothing to track.

Children

ThrowLab is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and under your control, you can view, export (via the iOS share sheet), or delete any recording at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes everything. There is no server-side data for us to access, correct, or delete on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
