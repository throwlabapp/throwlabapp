Frequently asked questions 

Do I need a special disc or extra hardware? 

No. ThrowLab works with every disc in your bag using your iPhone. There’s no sensor to purchase or attach, and no need to modify a disc. 

How accurate is the distance tracker? 

ThrowLab has been field tested to within one foot of the reference distance, including on wooded courses. 

How accurate are speed readings? 

ThrowLab’s speed readings have been tested against multiple radar devices, with results within a few mph. 

What part of the throw does ThrowLab measure? 

ThrowLab measures from release through early flight. More clear frames of the disc provide more measurement data. Flight drag and environmental conditions can contribute to slight differences from devices focused primarily on release. 

Can I record directly in ThrowLab? 

Yes. Choose Record Video on the Analyze screen to film in the app, or Upload Video to use an existing 240 fps slow-motion clip. 

Can I practice into a net? 

Yes. Leave enough room before the net to capture several clear frames of the disc in flight. You can also record in your backyard, in a field, or on the course. 

Can I save courses and disc plastics? 

Yes. Add a nearby course or enter one manually, and save each disc’s plastic in My Bag. Sessions can be filtered by course, and each physical disc keeps its own results. 

What do disc tilt and launch angle mean? 

Disc tilt describes the visible tilt of the disc’s rim. Launch angle describes whether it travels upward, level, or downward. A tilted disc can still travel on a level path. 

How are sessions grouped? 

Throws stay in the same session while the course remains unchanged and the gap between throws is under four hours. A course change or a gap of four hours or more starts a new session. 

What does the range in Flight Analysis mean? 

The range shows your lowest and highest saved readings for the same disc and throwing style, including this throw. The dot marks the current reading. The difference from average compares it with your earlier throws. 

Does it support forehand and left-handed throws? 

Yes. Choose forehand or backhand, right- or left-handed. 

How do I measure distance? 

Choose Measure Distance, tap Start at your throwing position, and follow the on-screen prompts as you walk to your disc. Tap Finish when you reach it. The result is the straight-line distance between the two positions. 

Are my videos uploaded? 

No. Throw videos are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded for analysis. No account is required.
