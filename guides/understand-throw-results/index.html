ThrowLab practice guide

How to read your ThrowLab analysis

ThrowLab evaluates speed, disc angle, and release path against a target for your selected disc, throwing style, and intended line. The gauges show relative ratings, rather than exact miles per hour or degree measurements.

What the three ratings mean

RatingWhat to look for
SpeedWhether the reading falls under, within, or over the target range for the selected throw.
Disc angleWhether the disc angle matches the intended straight, hyzer, or anhyzer line.
Release pathWhether the release travels down, level, or up relative to the target.

Track distance separately

Use Track distance to mark your release position, then walk to the disc and mark its final position. Distance is the estimate between these points, including skips or rolls. Qualifying throws contribute to personal bests and per-disc averages.

Separate release analysis from Compare

Analysis uses your recorded throw. Compare uses manufacturer flight ratings to estimate disc flight paths at the selected arm power tier and throwing style. Compare paths are not measured from your video. Wind, plastic, disc wear, and the actual throw can change flight.

What an unclear reading means

Camera position, lighting, blur, frame rate, and disc visibility can affect the result. An unclear reading should not be treated as proof that a target was missed. Review the recording setup before changing your technique based on uncertain evidence.

What counts as dialed in

A dialed-in throw has speed on target, a clear disc-angle reading that matches the intended line, and a level release path. Milestones track these throws by disc, throwing style, and line, with an overall total across the bag.
