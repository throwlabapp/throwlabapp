How to get more out of disc golf net practice

Record a throw into the net and check the flight readings after each set. A net can’t say how far a throw would have gone, so use it for how the disc leaves your hand and measure distance out on a field.

Set your phone up to the side and out of the line of fire. Discs bounce back off nets, so put the phone on something solid and well away from where the disc will land. The disc needs to stay in view for a few frames before it hits the net. The recording guide has the camera setup.

Throw a few, then look at speed, disc tilt, and launch angle. If one number holds steady and another jumps around, you know where to start. When you change your grip or release, change one thing and compare the next set with the last.

The net can’t give you distance, because every throw ends at the net. Head outside once in a while and use Measure Distance on real throws.
