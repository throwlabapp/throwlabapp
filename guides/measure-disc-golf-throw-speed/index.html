How to measure your disc golf throw speed

Record your throw in slow motion and let ThrowLab read the speed. You don’t need a radar gun or a sensor disc, and it works with any disc in your bag.

Why throw speed is worth knowing

Speed is the easiest number to chase and the easiest to misread. It tells you whether a change in your form actually did something, and it shows how your drivers, fairways, and mids compare when you throw them the same way.

A radar gun, a sensor disc, or your phone

A radar gun needs a second person to aim it and costs real money. A sensor disc puts the hardware inside one disc, so you’re measuring that disc and not the ones you actually play. Your phone is already in your pocket and can read a throw from slow-motion video, so there’s nothing to buy, attach, or charge.

Measure your speed with ThrowLab

Pick the disc you’re throwing, then record a throw in the app, or upload a slow-motion clip you already recorded. ThrowLab gives you speed along with disc tilt and launch angle for the same throw. For the best result, record from the side with the disc in view as it leaves your hand. The recording guide covers the setup.

Get numbers you can compare

One throw is a snapshot. A few throws with the same disc and the same throwing style show you your usual, and that’s the number to beat. Switching discs or styles changes the baseline, so compare like with like.

What speed doesn’t tell you

A faster throw isn’t always a longer one. Release angle, disc tilt, and wobble all change where the disc goes. That’s why ThrowLab shows them together. Here’s how to read the full analysis.
