ThrowLab practice guide

Disc golf throw analysis without a sensor disc

You can measure speed, disc tilt, and launch angle with just your phone. ThrowLab reads a slow-motion video of your throw, so there’s nothing to attach to a disc and nothing to charge.

What a sensor disc does

A sensor disc has electronics built into one disc, which measure the throw as it happens. It’s a real way to get data, and it comes with a cost and a limit: it measures that one disc, not the ones you actually use on the course.

What your phone can do instead

Record a throw in slow motion, or upload one you already recorded, and ThrowLab reads speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from the video. Distance is a separate measurement. You walk to your disc and it gives you the number, with no GPS drift.

Why using any disc matters

You can analyze the disc you actually throw, exactly as it is. Nothing gets added to it, and nothing changes how it feels or flies. Every disc keeps its own history, so you can compare your driver to your fairway driver, or one plastic to another.

Try it on a throw

Your first 5 analyses are free, flight or distance, and there’s no account to set up. Record a throw, check the three numbers, and see whether they tell you something you didn’t already know.
