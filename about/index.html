Hi, I’m Josh

Built between rounds in Austin.

I’ve lived in Austin and played disc golf for about 20 years. I still get out to the courses whenever I can, especially with my wife and kids. Apparently, that wasn’t quite enough disc golf, so I built an app, too.

I wanted to understand my throws and get more consistent, on and off the course, without buying expensive hardware or modifying a disc. More than that, I wanted to measure every disc in my bag, not just one training disc. ThrowLab grew from that idea: speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from an iPhone, plus a distance tracker I could trust in the trees.

Being part of the disc golf community is one of my favorite things about this. If ThrowLab helps someone make sense of a throw or enjoy practice a little more, that’s a good day. Have a question, an idea, or a course I should play? Say hello at josh@throwlabapp.com.
