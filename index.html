What it reads

Four numbers that actually change a throw

01

Power match

Is your release speed right for this mold, or are you under-powering a high-speed driver?

02

Disc angle

Hyzer, flat, or anhyzer measured at the moment the disc leaves your hand.

03

Release path

Flat, upward, or downward launch — the difference between a line and a lawn dart.

04

Predicted flight

Qualitative shape for your throw style: RHBH, RHFH, LHBH, or LHFH.
