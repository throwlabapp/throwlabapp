Flight analysis

Speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from one slow-motion throw.

Record or upload a slow-motion throw. ThrowLab reads your release and early flight and gives you three numbers to work with.

Speed

How fast the disc is moving, compared with your usual for that disc.

Disc tilt

Whether the rim is tilted down, flat, or up as the disc leaves your hand and starts flying.

Launch angle

Whether the disc is heading up, level, or down early in its flight.

Distance is measured separately, by walking to your disc.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a sensor disc or launch monitor?

No. ThrowLab is an app, not hardware. Nothing gets attached to your discs, so you can check your whole bag while every disc stays stock. Speed, disc tilt, and launch angle come from a slow-motion video of your throw.

Can I upload a video I already recorded?

Yes. Record a throw in ThrowLab, or upload a slow-motion video from Photos. Videos from the side, with the disc still in view after release, give the best readings.

Why is ThrowLab’s distance more accurate than a GPS app?

ThrowLab’s distance doesn’t drift the way a GPS reading does. Phone GPS can wander by many feet, especially under trees, while ThrowLab gives the same steady reading in the woods as in an open field. It has been field tested to within one foot of the reference distance, including on wooded courses.

What does ThrowLab measure?

Speed, disc tilt, and launch angle from your release and early flight, plus distance when you walk to your disc. Disc tilt is how far the disc’s rim is tilted up or down as it leaves your hand. Launch angle is whether the disc is heading up, level, or down early in its flight.

Does ThrowLab work with any disc?

Yes. Every disc in your bag works exactly as it is. There’s no sensor to buy or attach, nothing to modify, and each disc keeps its own history.

What iPhone do I need?

iOS 17 or later, and an iPhone that records 240 fps slow motion. You can record in ThrowLab or upload a slow-motion clip from Photos. Distance measurement works on any supported iPhone.
