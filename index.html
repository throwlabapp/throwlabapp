What ThrowLab analyzes

Three readings that explain your release.

ThrowLab analyzes a slow-motion clip to show whether your speed fits the disc, how the disc is angled at release, and whether it is traveling upward, flat, or downward. Then it explains how those pieces are likely to shape the flight.

01

Power match

See whether your release speed is slightly low, matched, or slightly high for the disc you selected. A driver and a midrange should not be judged the same way.

02

Disc angle

See whether the disc leaves your hand on anhyzer, slightly anhyzer, flat, slightly hyzer, or hyzer. ThrowLab interprets the angle for your throwing side and throw style.

03

Release path

See whether the disc is traveling upward, slightly upward, flat, slightly downward, or downward immediately after release.

Scored to the disc you threw

Common questions

Questions players ask before analyzing a throw.

What does a disc golf analysis app measure?

ThrowLab estimates power match, disc angle, and release path from slow-motion video. It combines those readings with the selected disc’s flight ratings and throw style to describe the likely flight and suggest what to change next.

What do hyzer, flat, and anhyzer mean in disc golf?

On a hyzer release, the disc’s outer edge—the edge farthest from your body—is lower than the inner edge. On an anhyzer release, the outer edge is higher. A flat release sits between them. ThrowLab interprets the angle using your throwing side and whether you throw backhand or forehand.

Does ThrowLab work for forehand and left-handed throws?

Yes. ThrowLab supports right-hand backhand, right-hand forehand, left-hand backhand, and left-hand forehand. Your selected throw style helps the app interpret hyzer and anhyzer correctly and describe the expected flight direction.

Can I use ThrowLab when throwing into a net?

Yes. ThrowLab reads the release and early flight, so the disc does not need to land on camera. Keep the disc visible for several frames after it leaves your hand.

Do I need a radar gun, sensor disc, or stickers?

No. ThrowLab uses slow-motion video from your iPhone. You select the disc from your bag and center it in one mid-flight frame before analysis.

Why does the selected disc matter?

Different molds are designed for different speeds and flight shapes. ThrowLab uses speed and category for power matching, while turn and fade help explain the predicted flight and disc-fit guidance.

Is ThrowLab analyzing my body mechanics?

No. ThrowLab is focused on the disc at and immediately after release. It does not score reach-back, footwork, brace, or hip rotation.

Are videos uploaded to a server?

No. Throw videos are analyzed on your iPhone and are not uploaded for analysis. ThrowLab does not require an account.
