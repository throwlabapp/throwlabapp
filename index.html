Frequently asked questions

What does ThrowLab analyze?

ThrowLab analyzes speed, disc angle, and release path from slow-motion video, and separately tracks throw distance using two GPS marks. Release gauges show relative ratings against target ranges rather than exact miles per hour or degree measurements.

How does distance tracking work?

Mark your throw position, walk to where the disc stopped, and tap Mark landing. ThrowLab estimates straight-line distance between those GPS marks, including skips or rolls. It shows GPS quality and elevation when available. Qualifying throws count toward recorded bests, distance goals, and averages; measurements outside the quality or distance limits can be saved in Sessions without counting toward records or trends.

How does progress tracking work?

Only throws with the same disc, throwing style, and intended line are compared. ThrowLab tracks target hits for speed, disc angle, and release path separately.

How are practice sessions created?

Throws made within the same practice window are grouped automatically. You can have more than one session in a day without using a start or stop button.

What counts toward a milestone?

A dialed-in throw has speed on target, a clear disc-angle reading that matches your line, and a level release path. Goals are tracked by disc, throwing style, and line. Your overall total counts across the bag.

Are Compare flight paths measured from my videos?

No. Compare estimates paths from manufacturer flight ratings. You can overlay up to four discs and choose an arm power tier and throwing style. Actual flight changes with the throw, wind, plastic, and disc wear.

Does it support forehand and left-handed throws?

Yes. ThrowLab supports RHBH, RHFH, LHBH, and LHFH, plus straight, hyzer, and anhyzer intended lines.

Can I use it with a practice net?

Yes, for release analysis. The disc does not need to land on camera. Keep it visible for several frames after release. GPS distance tracking is intended for throws where you can mark the release and final disc positions.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Throw videos are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded for analysis. No account is required.

How much does ThrowLab Pro cost?

New users get 5 free video analyses and 5 free saved distance measurements. ThrowLab Pro is then $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year for unlimited analyses and distance measurements.
